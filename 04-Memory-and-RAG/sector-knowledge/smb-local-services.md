# Sector Knowledge Base: SMB Local Services

## Sector Overview

SMB Local Services covers small to medium businesses that provide hands-on services to homeowners and local commercial properties. These businesses are typically owner-operated or have 2-50 employees, serve a geographic radius of 20-50 miles, and rely heavily on local search visibility and reputation to generate business.

This is OnTrack Marketing's primary sector and the most developed knowledge base. It directly integrates with the Rise Local 15-signal pain scoring pipeline.

## Industry Pain Points

These are documented in the language that business owners actually use. Each pain point maps to a Rise Local signal and a recommended solution.

### Discovery & Visibility Pain Points

**"I need more calls"**
- Translation: Not enough inbound leads from organic channels.
- Root cause: Poor Google Business Profile optimization, low local SEO, no review strategy.
- Rise Local signal: Low call volume trend (Signal #3).
- Solution: GBP optimization + review generation campaign + local SEO.

**"My website doesn't rank"**
- Translation: Business does not appear in Google search results for relevant terms.
- Root cause: No SEO, thin content, poor technical health, no backlinks.
- Rise Local signal: Low organic search visibility (Signal #7).
- Solution: Technical SEO audit + local content strategy + citation building.

**"I'm losing to competitors on Google Maps"**
- Translation: Competitors appear in the Local 3-Pack; this business does not.
- Root cause: Fewer reviews, inconsistent NAP, less GBP activity, weaker local signals.
- Rise Local signal: GBP ranking position (Signal #1), review count/velocity (Signal #2).
- Solution: GBP audit + review acceleration + local link building + post frequency.

**"I don't know where my leads are coming from"**
- Translation: No call tracking, no attribution, spending money but can't measure ROI.
- Root cause: No analytics setup, no call tracking numbers, no CRM.
- Rise Local signal: No call tracking detected (Signal #12).
- Solution: Call tracking setup + Google Analytics + CRM implementation.

### Reputation Pain Points

**"I got a bad review and don't know what to do"**
- Translation: Negative review is hurting perception; no response strategy.
- Root cause: No review management process.
- Rise Local signal: Recent negative reviews (Signal #4).
- Solution: Review response templates + proactive review generation to bury the negative.

**"My competitor has way more reviews than me"**
- Translation: Low review count relative to local competitors.
- Root cause: No systematic review ask process.
- Rise Local signal: Review count gap vs competitors (Signal #2).
- Solution: Automated review request flows (SMS + email post-service).

### Advertising Pain Points

**"I tried Google Ads and it didn't work"**
- Translation: Ran self-managed Google Ads, burned budget with poor targeting or landing pages.
- Root cause: Broad match keywords, no negative keywords, sending traffic to homepage, no conversion tracking.
- Rise Local signal: Low Google Ads quality score (Signal #9), high CPC detected (Signal #10).
- Solution: Rebuild campaigns with proper structure + dedicated landing pages + call tracking.

**"I'm paying for leads but they're junk"**
- Translation: Using lead aggregators (HomeAdvisor, Angi) and getting shared/low-quality leads.
- Root cause: Over-reliance on third-party lead sources.
- Rise Local signal: High third-party lead spend (Signal #11).
- Solution: Shift budget to owned channels (Google Ads, LSA, SEO) for exclusive leads.

### Operational Pain Points

**"I'm too busy to deal with marketing"**
- Translation: Owner is a technician first, marketer last. No time for marketing activities.
- Root cause: One-person operation wearing all hats.
- Solution: Done-for-you marketing packages, not DIY tools.

**"I had a bad experience with a marketing company"**
- Translation: Previous agency overpromised and underdelivered, no transparency.
- Root cause: Burned trust, agency did not report or deliver measurable results.
- Solution: Transparent reporting, clear KPIs, month-to-month contracts, show results early.

### KPIs to Report Monthly

1. **Total leads generated** (calls + form fills + chats)
2. **Cost per lead** (total ad spend / total leads)
3. **Lead-to-appointment rate** (leads that became booked jobs)
4. **Cost per acquisition** (total marketing spend / new customers)
5. **Revenue attributed to marketing** (if CRM tracks this)
6. **Google Business Profile metrics** (views, clicks, calls, direction requests)
7. **Review count and average rating** (monthly delta)
8. **Website traffic** (organic, paid, direct, referral)
9. **Google Ads metrics** (impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, conversions)
10. **Search ranking positions** (target keywords, Local 3-Pack)

## Compliance and Regulatory Requirements

### Texas-Specific (Primary Market)

| Requirement | Details | Marketing Impact |
|------------|---------|-----------------|
| **TDLR Licensing** | Texas Dept of Licensing - plumbers, electricians, HVAC must be licensed | Must display license number in ads |
| **Contractor Registration** | Some cities require separate registration | Verify city requirements before advertising |
| **BBB Ratings** | Not legally required but impacts trust | Can use BBB rating in marketing if accurate |
| **Insurance** | General liability required for most trades | "Licensed & Insured" is a key trust signal |
| **Home Solicitation Act** | Cancellation rights for in-home sales >$25 | Affects door-to-door marketing |

### Advertising-Specific

- **Google LSA:** Requires background check and license verification. Plumbers, HVAC, and electricians must be licensed.
- **Facebook Ads:** No special restrictions for home services, but avoid health/safety claims.
- **Google Ads:** Cannot use misleading emergency claims ("24/7" must actually mean 24/7).
- **Reviews:** Cannot offer incentives for reviews (violates Google and FTC guidelines). Can ask, but cannot pay or discount.
- **Guarantees:** "Satisfaction guaranteed" must have a defined policy. Avoid unsubstantiated claims.
