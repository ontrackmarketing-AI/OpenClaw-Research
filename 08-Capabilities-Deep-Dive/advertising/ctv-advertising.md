# CTV Advertising: The Trade Desk vs Amazon DSP vs StackAdapt

## Overview

Connected TV (CTV) advertising delivers video ads to viewers on streaming platforms (Hulu, Peacock, Paramount+, Pluto TV, etc.) through internet-connected devices (smart TVs, Roku, Fire TV, Apple TV). CTV combines the visual impact of traditional TV advertising with the targeting precision and measurability of digital advertising.

For SW Recovery Services, CTV advertising can build brand awareness and credibility among high-value audiences in specific geographic markets, complementing the digital retargeting efforts on Google, Meta, and LinkedIn. CTV campaigns typically serve as top-of-funnel brand awareness, driving website visits that feed into the retargeting ecosystem.

**Key consideration**: CTV requires higher minimum budgets than social/search advertising and is best suited for established businesses ready to invest in brand-building alongside direct response channels.

## Platform Comparison

### The Trade Desk (TTD)

**Overview**: Enterprise-grade demand-side platform (DSP) widely considered the industry leader in programmatic CTV advertising. Offers the broadest inventory access and most sophisticated targeting, but requires significant expertise or agency support.

**CTV Inventory Access**:
- Premium streaming: Hulu, Peacock, Paramount+, Pluto TV, Tubi, Discovery+
- App-based: Roku Channel, Samsung TV Plus, LG Channels, Vizio WatchFree
- Sports: ESPN+, Fox Sports, NBC Sports
- News: CNN, Fox News, MSNBC streaming
- Open internet inventory across 100+ publishers

**Targeting Capabilities**:
- First-party data onboarding (CRM lists)
- Third-party data segments (demographics, interests, purchase intent)
- Contextual targeting (content genre, show-level)
- Geographic targeting (DMA, zip code, address-level)
- Household-level targeting via IP matching
- Cross-device identity resolution (TV to mobile/desktop)
- Unified ID 2.0 for cookieless targeting

**Measurement & Attribution**:
- Household-level attribution (ad exposure to website visit/conversion)
- Brand lift studies
- Foot traffic attribution (for local businesses)
- Cross-channel attribution with digital campaigns
- Real-time reporting dashboard

**Self-Serve vs Managed**:
- Self-serve platform available but requires training
- Most users operate through agency partners
- Platform fee: typically 10-15% of media spend (take rate)

**Minimum Budget**: No officially published minimum, but industry consensus is $2,500-$3,000/month minimum for meaningful testing at the local/regional level.

### Amazon DSP

**Overview**: Amazon's demand-side platform with exclusive access to Amazon's streaming inventory (Prime Video, Freevee, Twitch) and Amazon's massive first-party shopping data. Growing rapidly as Amazon expands its ad-supported content.

**CTV Inventory Access**:
- Exclusive: Amazon Prime Video ads (launched Jan 2024), Freevee, Twitch
- Exclusive partnership: Roku inventory via Amazon DSP (80M+ US CTV households, Q4 2025)
- Amazon Publisher Direct: curated third-party inventory
- Third-party exchanges for broader reach

**Key Competitive Advantage (2025-2026)**:
- Amazon + Roku exclusive integration announced June 2025
- Reach ~80% of US CTV households exclusively through Amazon DSP
- This is a significant differentiator vs The Trade Desk and StackAdapt
- Amazon's closed-loop measurement connects ad exposure to purchase behavior

**Targeting Capabilities**:
- Amazon shopping data (purchase history, browsing, intent signals)
- Lifestyle segments (based on Amazon purchasing patterns)
- In-market audiences (actively shopping for relevant categories)
- Geographic, demographic, and interest-based targeting
- Advertiser audiences (CRM upload for retargeting)
- Lookalike audiences based on Amazon purchase data

**Measurement & Attribution**:
- Closed-loop attribution: ad view to Amazon purchase
- Brand lift studies
- Reach and frequency reporting
- Cross-device reporting
- New Customer metrics for Amazon sellers

**Self-Serve vs Managed**:
- Self-service: $10,000 recommended campaign minimum
- Managed service: $50,000 campaign minimum
- Reseller access available starting at $5,000/month
- No platform fee for self-service (media cost only)
- Amazon recently undercut TTD on take rates (margin wars of 2025)

**Minimum Budget**: $10,000 self-service, $50,000 managed, $5,000 via resellers.

### StackAdapt

**Overview**: Self-serve DSP designed for agencies and mid-market advertisers. Known for ease of use, AI-driven optimization, and multi-channel support (native, display, video, CTV, audio, DOOH). Best option for teams without deep programmatic expertise.

**CTV Inventory Access**:
- Major streaming apps and FAST channels
- Roku, Fire TV, Apple TV, Samsung Smart TV inventory
- Third-party exchange inventory
- Less exclusive premium inventory than TTD or Amazon

**Targeting Capabilities**:
- Behavioral targeting (browsing and content consumption)
- Contextual targeting (content category)
- Geographic targeting (DMA, state, zip code)
- First-party data onboarding (CRM lists)
- Third-party data segments
- Lookalike modeling
- Intent-based targeting

**Measurement & Attribution**:
- Post-view and post-click attribution
- Conversion tracking via pixel
- Reach and frequency reporting
- Brand awareness lift measurement
- Cross-channel performance comparison

**Self-Serve Platform**:
- Purpose-built self-serve interface (most user-friendly of the three)
- AI-driven campaign optimization
- Multi-channel campaign management from single dashboard
- No massive minimum spend requirements
- Platform fee: typically 15% of media spend or CPM markup
- Competitor separation and frequency capping for CTV

**Minimum Budget**: No published minimum spend requirement. Most accessible of the three platforms for small-to-mid-sized advertisers.

## CTV CPM Benchmarks (2025-2026)

| Inventory Source | CPM Range | Notes |
|-----------------|-----------|-------|
| Programmatic standard | $20-$25 | Base CPMs, settling range |
| Premium targeting + placement | $40-$60 | Specific audience targeting |
| Netflix (programmatic) | $20-$30 | Newer ad-supported tier |
| Hulu | $10-$30 | Varies by targeting |
| Amazon Prime Video | $25-$60 | Premium inventory |
| Peacock | $15-$35 | Programmatic inventory |
| Roku Channel | $15-$25 | Large reach |
| Free ad-supported (FAST) | $10-$20 | Tubi, Pluto TV, etc. |
| Local/geo-targeted | $25-$45 | DMA or zip code targeting |

**Average CTV CPM trend**: Base CPMs are stabilizing around $20-$25 with premium segments commanding $40-$60. Compared to traditional TV ($10-$30 CPM for broad reach), CTV costs more but delivers precise targeting and measurable attribution.

## Implementation Approach

### Phase 1: Platform Selection and Setup (Week 1)

1. **Platform recommendation for SW Recovery**:
   - **Primary: StackAdapt** -- No minimum spend, easiest self-serve, good for testing CTV without major budget commitment
   - **Secondary: Amazon DSP** -- If budget allows ($5K+/month), access to exclusive Roku/Prime inventory covering 80%+ of CTV households
   - **Future: The Trade Desk** -- When scaling to $5K+/month with agency support

2. **Account setup**
   - Create advertiser account on chosen platform
   - Set up billing and payment method
   - Install conversion pixel on SW Recovery website
   - Upload CRM audience lists (hashed emails)

### Phase 2: Creative Production (Week 2)

3. **Video ad production**
   - :15 second spot (most common CTV format)
   - :30 second spot (for storytelling/testimonial)
   - Non-skippable format (standard for CTV)
   - Professional production quality expected (TV-level)
   - Include clear CTA and brand identification
   - Comply with platform creative specifications

4. **Creative specifications**
   | Spec | Requirement |
   |------|------------|
   | Resolution | 1920x1080 (HD) minimum, 3840x2160 (4K) preferred |
   | Aspect ratio | 16:9 |
   | File format | MP4, MOV |
   | File size | Under 1GB |
   | Duration | :15, :30, :60 seconds |
   | Audio | Stereo, -24 LUFS loudness |
   | Frame rate | 23.976, 29.97, or 59.94 fps |

### Phase 3: Campaign Launch (Week 3)

5. **Targeting strategy**
   ```
   CTV Campaign: SW Recovery Brand Awareness
   ├── Geo targeting: Primary service markets (DMA or zip code)
   ├── Audience: Business decision-makers
   │   ├── Demographic: HHI $100K+
   │   ├── Interest: Business, finance, legal
   │   └── Behavioral: Business content consumers
   ├── Frequency cap: 3-5 impressions per household per week
   └── Dayparting: Primetime (7 PM - 11 PM) and weekends
   ```

6. **Campaign settings**
   - Flight dates: 4-week minimum test
   - Pacing: Even delivery across flight
   - Frequency cap: 3-5 per household per week
   - Companion banners: Display retargeting pixel

### Phase 4: Measurement and Optimization (Week 4+)

7. **KPIs to track**
   | KPI | Target | Notes |
   |-----|--------|-------|
   | Completion rate | 90%+ | CTV is non-skippable |
   | Reach | 10K-50K households | Depends on budget/geo |
   | Frequency | 3-5x per household | Per 4-week flight |
   | Website lift | 10-20% increase | From CTV-exposed households |
   | Brand search lift | 5-15% increase | Branded search queries |
   | CPM | $20-$35 | Programmatic standard |

8. **Optimization levers**
   - Adjust geo targeting based on response
   - Shift budget to higher-performing dayparts
   - Test different creative versions
   - Expand or narrow audience segments
   - Adjust frequency caps based on diminishing returns
